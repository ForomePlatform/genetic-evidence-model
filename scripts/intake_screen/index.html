Screen (protocol §6.4)

Read the paper, then answer the four gates. Screen with no AI assistance (protocol §8.1); ordinary literature databases are allowed. A fail must name the failed gate with evidence. Outcomes are final once recorded. Use one browser and one tab for screening.

Gates (criteria 1 to 4; all four must hold to pass)
1. Primary research article in English.
2. Can be extracted into usable canonical text.The full text opens and is machine-readable.
3. Contains at least two separate claims linking a genetic entity to a phenotype.
4. In scope, or deliberately adjacent to GEM’s current scope.
